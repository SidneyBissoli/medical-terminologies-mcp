## Decision (mcp-builder gap closed)

Zero cross-cluster confusion → **keep the current tool names; no rename.**
The "prefix by terminology" gap of the mcp-builder audit is closed by
evidence: the terminology prefixes already in place (`icd11_*`, `cid10_*`,
`loinc_*`, `rxnorm_*`, `atc_*`, `mesh_*`, `map_*`/`validate_codes`/
`find_equivalent`, `terminology_*`) are sufficient selection signal.
Renaming would be breaking for zero measured benefit. Do not reopen
without new evidence (e.g. a future eval showing systematic confusion).
